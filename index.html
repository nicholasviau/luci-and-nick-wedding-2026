Luciana & Nicholas

February 7, 2026 — Key Biscayne, Florida

It was a Friday night and the Glitter Boys were spinning records at the Sinclair in Cambridge. We all know Luci likes to dance. And we also know that she can be a real… smooth talker. One of the DJs was super cute but stayed behind the table the entire night. Hmph!

At the end of the night, Luci walked right up to him with her eyebrows furrowed and asked, “Why don’t you dance?” With a mix of surprise and awe the DJ responded, “Well somebody needs to play the records. If you come back next week, we’ll dance.”

She came back. We danced.

We all knew it would come to this. Now, over six years later, we’re bringing together our families and some of our dearest friends to watch us make it official and celebrate. We hope you’ll join us.

We've created this site as your guide to the weekend. Here you'll find all the details about the event itself, as well as helpful information about Miami and the surrounding area. Many of you are traveling from out of town, and we really appreciate it. We've included plenty of practical information below to help make your trip as smooth and enjoyable as possible.

— Luciana & Nicholas

Attire

What should you wear? Great question. Wear something that makes you feel beautiful. It's not black tie and it's not casual—think party attire! Dress up, look good, and have fun with it. Boys, you don't need to wear a tie, but please skip the shorts and sleeveless shirts.

Miami in February is typically high 70s to low 80s during the day, dipping into the 60s at night. The clubhouse is open-air and right on the ocean, so plan for a light layer in case of a breezy evening.

Lodging

Although the wedding is in Key Biscayne, the island is small and mostly residential, so lodging can be limited. We suggest staying in Miami proper for more options. The beach club is about a 15-minute drive from central Miami, where most hotels are located.

We’ve also curated Airbnb options for both solo travelers and groups. Locations vary, so consider what you want nearby when you book. Click here and check it out!

Recommended neighborhoods:

  • Key Biscayne – Wedding location. Great for a relaxed vibe and the shortest ride to the venue, though hotel options are more limited
  • Coconut Grove – Our personal favorite. It's leafy, walkable, and full of cafés and restaurants.
  • Brickell – Miami’s business district and the closest neighborhood to Key Biscayne. Lots of high-rises (many with Airbnbs) and a central location.
  • Wynwood – Miami’s arts district. Great for daytime exploring with murals, galleries, and other spaces.

Rideshare (Lyft and Uber) is generally more affordable here than in cities like New York or Boston. In our experience, Lyft often runs ~20% less than Uber in Miami.

Have questions about where to stay or what to do? Text or email us—happy to help!
