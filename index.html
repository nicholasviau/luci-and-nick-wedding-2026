Attire

What should you wear? Great question. Some say “semi-formal,” but we think sophisticated-yet-casual is always a good choice. Be comfortable, but look good doing it. Boys, you don’t need to wear a tie, but please no shorts or sleeveless shirts.

Miami in February is typically high 70s to low 80s during the day, dipping into the 60s at night. The clubhouse is open-air and right on the ocean, so plan for a light layer in case of a breezy evening.

Lodging

Although the wedding is in Key Biscayne, the island is small and mostly residential, so lodging can be limited. We suggest staying in Miami proper for more options. The beach club is about a 15-minute drive from central Miami, where most hotels are located.

We’ve also curated Airbnb options for both solo travelers and groups. Locations vary, so consider what you want nearby when you book.

Recommended neighborhoods:

  • Key Biscayne – Wedding location. Great for a relaxed vibe and the shortest ride to the venue, though hotel options are more limited
  • Coconut Grove – Our personal favorite—leafy, walkable, and full of cafés and restaurants.
  • Brickell – The closest neighborhood to Key Biscayne and Miami’s business district. Lots of high-rises (many with Airbnbs) and a central location.
  • Wynwood – Miami’s arts district. Great for daytime exploring with murals, galleries, and other spaces.

Rideshare (Lyft and Uber) is generally more affordable here than in cities like New York or Boston. In our experience, Lyft often runs ~20% less than Uber in Miami.

Have questions about where to stay or what to do? Text or email us—happy to help!
